About Twinline Exteriors

Twinline Exteriors Corporation is a hands-on exterior cladding team with 8+ years of experience in siding and facade installation. We take on projects of any complexity and keep the process simple: clear scope, clean details, and dependable timelines.

Our portfolio includes dozens of completed projects across Canada and Europe — from residential renovations to commercial and multi-unit builds. We focus on straight lines, tight finishes, and exterior systems built to handle tough weather.

Need a refresh? We also handle siding renovations — removing old cladding (scope-based) and installing a new, modern exterior system.

What you can expect

  • Professional workmanship and clean detailing
  • Fast turnaround and on-schedule delivery
  • Commercial + residential experience
  • Safety-first approach on every jobsite
  • Workmanship warranty (scope-based)
