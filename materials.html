Materials

Materials We Work With

We install and renovate most exterior cladding systems commonly used in Calgary and throughout Alberta — for both commercial and residential projects. Exception: we do not take stone or brick/masonry work.

Cladding & siding

  • Fiber cement siding & panels (James Hardie-style systems)
  • Vinyl siding
  • Engineered wood siding
  • Composite cladding systems
  • Natural wood siding (when specified)
  • Metal cladding: corrugated/ribbed panels, architectural sheets, trims
  • Modern panel systems: ACM (aluminum composite), HPL facade panels, rainscreen panels
  • Stucco / EIFS (when specified as part of an exterior package)

Finishing & protection

  • Soffit & fascia systems (fiber cement or metal options)
  • Trim packages, corners, window/door trims, reveals
  • Flashings, cappings, drip edges, and clean transitions
  • Housewrap and weather barrier detailing (as specified)
  • Furring/strapping and ventilated rainscreen assemblies
