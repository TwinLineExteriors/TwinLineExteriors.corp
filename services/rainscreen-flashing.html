Rainscreen, Flashing & Trim

High-quality water management details: rainscreen assemblies, strapping/furring, flashings, and trims that protect the building envelope and keep the facade looking sharp.

What’s included

  • Furring / strapping for ventilated rainscreen assemblies
  • Flashings at windows, doors, transitions, and penetrations
  • Cappings and drip edges for clean water-shedding details
  • Integration with housewrap / weather barrier layers (as specified)
  • Precise finishing: corners, reveals, seams, and joints
  • Coordination with other trades on penetrations
